**Short description**

A game about dying, not boobs (sorry). Tizzy has to get out of a room full of goblins.

**Description**

Tizzy wakes up in a walled-in room with a dozen goblins and a locked door. Somewhere in the room is the key, and a few things worth wearing. None of the goblins are friendly.

**How it works**

- Walk into a goblin to pick a fight. Walk away from one and it gets a free swing.
- Your attack and defence are handfuls of dice that only count when they come up six, so a fight can drag on. Gear wears out and breaks.
- Goblins drop what they carry when they go down. Meat heals. Some of the meat is rotten.
- Find the key, unlock the door, walk through it. Your dignity score is what you take away.
- Save to any of five slots from the game menu.

**Controls**

Arrow keys, WASD or the numpad to move and pick menu items. Space or Enter is A (confirm), Escape is B (back). On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.
